# Evenin' Shift

> Comedy horror set in Somerset. You're on the night shift again. Look lively, you get all sorts turning up round these parts. Let the right ones in, send the wronguns on their way and try not to make any enemies— there are strange folk about.

---

## Decide who enters the premises and who does not.

You take on the role of Jack, a seasoned security guard working in a quiet village in Somerset in the early 90s.
